## Adapter scope

Live ingestion stores the exact official `GEN/01/REV1` workbook with SHA-256
`98e34d9b65b53858cd08a57a566e45050b08093ad85ba5714fe6fbd78055ae6d`.
The committed network-independent fixture contains only the attributed World
aggregate fields needed by this product for every year from 1950 through 2025.
Years 1950–2023 come from the workbook's `Estimates` sheet. Years 2024–2025
come from its `Medium variant` sheet and must be displayed as modeled
projections, not observed or final values.
